const float scale_x,

noexcept

Set mouse relative mapping to another coord space.

Make mouse_x()/mouse_y() report positions inside a scaled image drawn at (x, y) in screen coordinates with the given scale, for example a letterboxed or integer-scaled render target. See present_scaled_target(). Positions over the bars fall outside the image's coordinate range.

Parameters
xUpper X position of space.
yUpper Y position of space.
scale_xScale "width".
scale_yScale "height".
